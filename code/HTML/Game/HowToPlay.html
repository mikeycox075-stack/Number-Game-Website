How to Play

Here are the Rules

1. Select the Minimun Number your players can choose

2. Then select the Maximum Number your players can choose

3. Then pick the amout of guesses your players can have per round

4. The each player must select their numbers withought looking at eachothers numbers

5. Then Simply attempt to guess eachothers number, player with the lowest amount of guesses wins!
